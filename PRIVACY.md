# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** December 28, 2025

## Overview

Just The Answer ("the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

## Data Collection

### What We Collect

Just The Answer collects **minimal data** and operates with complete transparency:

1. **Local Settings Only**
   - Extension enabled/disabled state
   - Stored in Chrome's local storage
   - Never transmitted anywhere
   - Can be deleted by clearing browser data

2. **No Personal Information**
   - We do NOT collect email addresses
   - We do NOT collect user names or IDs
   - We do NOT track browsing history
   - We do NOT collect IP addresses

3. **No Content Tracking**
   - We do NOT read or store your chat content
   - We do NOT analyze the answers you copy
   - We do NOT track which websites you visit
   - We do NOT use analytics or tracking cookies

### How We Use Information

The only data we process is:
- **Copy operations** - The text you manually copy is sent to your system clipboard (not to us)
- **Button rendering** - DOM analysis on the current page to show copy buttons (in-browser only)
- **Storage** - Your extension enabled/disabled preference (local only)

## Permissions Explained

The extension requests the following Chrome permissions:

### `clipboardWrite`
- **Purpose:** Allows the extension to copy content to your clipboard
- **How it's used:** When you click a copy button, the selected content is copied to your system clipboard
- **Your control:** You can paste anywhere you choose; copied data is never sent to external servers

### `storage`
- **Purpose:** Stores your extension settings (enabled/disabled state)
- **How it's used:** Remembers whether you turned the extension on or off
- **Your control:** This data is stored locally on your device in Chrome's local storage

## No Third-Party Services

This extension:
- Does NOT use Google Analytics or similar tracking
- Does NOT send data to external servers
- Does NOT contain ads or ad networks
- Does NOT sell your data to third parties
- Does NOT use cookies for tracking

## Data Security

All operations happen locally on your browser:
- Content copying happens in your browser memory
- Settings are stored in Chrome local storage on your device
- No data transmission = no data breach risk
- You have complete control over what you copy

## Your Rights

You have complete control:

### Access Your Data
- All extension data is visible in Chrome's settings
- Go to `chrome://extensions/` → Just The Answer → Details

### Delete Your Data
- Clear extension data: Settings → Privacy and security → Clear browsing data
- Select "Cookies and other site data"
- The extension will reset to default settings

### Disable the Extension
- Click the extension icon and toggle "Enable Extension" OFF
- Or uninstall from Chrome Web Store or `chrome://extensions/`

## Children's Privacy

This extension is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
